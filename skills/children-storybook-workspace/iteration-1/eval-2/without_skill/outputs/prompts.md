# Illustration prompts — planned, not generated

Use the character definitions in characters.md verbatim for every prompt. All images are MISSING. Generate illustration-only images without text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks. One portrait image per face, 1024 × 1400; consistent watercolor style. Keep characters and key details inside x=130–894. Reserve y=1050–1400 as a calm cream text zone on interiors; no critical art there. Covers reserve y=70–460 for title. Do not mirror back-side art: the renderer handles back-side UV orientation.

## Front cover

Pip and Dot sit on dry grass watching one leaf on a pale blue shallow pond; Mama beside them; Pip curious but cautious. Leave upper third open for title.

## Page 01

Pip sits on a broad dry grassy bank beside a tiny sheltered shallow inlet; relaxed adult bear nearby, small friend at respectful distance; visible bottom.

## Page 02

Pip and Dot sit side by side well back from water watching one floating leaf; Mama within arm’s reach, no urging gestures.

## Page 03

Pip seated securely on dry flat bank voluntarily reaches one paw to calm water surface; Mama supports his other paw, Dot watches patiently.

## Page 04

## Page 05

## Page 06
