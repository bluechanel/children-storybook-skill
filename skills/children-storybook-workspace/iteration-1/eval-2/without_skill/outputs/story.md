# Pip, One Little Step

English picture-book draft for ages 4. Planning only; all artwork is missing.

## Front cover

Pip, One Little Step

## Page 1 — Watching Together

Pip sat by the pond.  
The water went ripple, ripple.  
Pip held his paws close.  
“I’m not ready,” he said.

## Page 2 — Watching Together

“We can just watch,” said Dot.  
Dot sat beside him.  
Mama stayed close.  
A leaf bobbed by.

## Page 3 — A Little Touch

“I want to try one paw,” said Pip.  
Mama held his other paw.  
Tap.  
The water felt cool.

## Page 4 — A Little Touch

Pip pulled his paw back.  
“That’s enough for now.”  
“Okay,” said Dot.  
They watched the little rings.

## Page 5 — My Own Small Step

After a while, Pip smiled.  
“I want to try my toes.”  
Mama held his paw.  
Pip chose one small step.

## Page 6 — My Own Small Step

Pip wiggled his toes.  
Dot floated a leaf nearby.  
“I like this little bit,” said Pip.  
“Let’s play here.”

## Back cover

One little step.  
In my own time.
