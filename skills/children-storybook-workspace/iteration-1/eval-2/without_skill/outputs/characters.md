# Character settings

- **Pip:** young small honey-brown bear with round ears, cream oval muzzle, dark brown eyes, tiny black nose and short rounded paws; plain sky-blue sleeveless top, bare feet. Keeps the same clothing and proportions. Starts hesitant and remains free to stop throughout.
- **Dot:** small russet rabbit friend, cream muzzle, short round tail, two upright ears, plain muted coral neckerchief. Roughly shoulder-high to seated Pip. Patient; never pulls, pushes, splashes or bargains.
- **Mama:** adult warm-brown bear, cream muzzle, plain moss-green cardigan; about twice Pip’s standing height. Calm and attentive, always nearby, holding his paw only when he chooses to touch or step into water.
- **Setting:** same sheltered pond inlet in daylight, broad flat grassy bank, sandy shallow edge with visible bottom, one floating leaf. No deep-water play or distant adult.
- **Style:** gentle watercolor, warm cream paper, soft edges, washed sage greens and pale blue water, uncluttered compositions, legible faces and paws. Keep character colors, anatomy, accessories and sizes fixed.
