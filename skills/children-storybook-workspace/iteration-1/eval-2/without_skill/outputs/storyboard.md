# Page-by-page storyboard

Each theme occupies one open spread. Odd pages are left; even pages are right. Faces and essential actions stay away from the inner gutter.

## Page 1 · spread 1 · left · Watching Together

Pip sits on a broad dry grassy bank beside a tiny sheltered shallow inlet; relaxed adult bear nearby, small friend at respectful distance; visible bottom.

Text in reserved lower panel:

Pip sat by the pond.  
The water went ripple, ripple.  
Pip held his paws close.  
“I’m not ready,” he said.

## Page 2 · spread 1 · right · Watching Together

Pip and Dot sit side by side well back from water watching one floating leaf; Mama within arm’s reach, no urging gestures.

Text in reserved lower panel:

“We can just watch,” said Dot.  
Dot sat beside him.  
Mama stayed close.  
A leaf bobbed by.

## Page 3 · spread 2 · left · A Little Touch

Pip seated securely on dry flat bank voluntarily reaches one paw to calm water surface; Mama supports his other paw, Dot watches patiently.

Text in reserved lower panel:

“I want to try one paw,” said Pip.  
Mama held his other paw.  
Tap.  
The water felt cool.

## Page 4 · spread 2 · right · A Little Touch

Pip rests both paws on his lap on dry bank; tiny ripples spread where he touched water; Dot calmly watches, Mama close.

Text in reserved lower panel:

Pip pulled his paw back.  
“That’s enough for now.”  
“Okay,” said Dot.  
They watched the little rings.

## Page 5 · spread 3 · left · My Own Small Step

Pip chooses a careful step into a broad clear ankle-deep sandy margin with Mama holding paw beside him; no drop-off, no jumping, Dot remains on bank.

Text in reserved lower panel:

After a while, Pip smiled.  
“I want to try my toes.”  
Mama held his paw.  
Pip chose one small step.

## Page 6 · spread 3 · right · My Own Small Step

Pip stands ankle-deep holding Mama’s paw, content and a little tentative, guiding a floating leaf with free paw; Dot crouches on safe dry bank; no swimming.

Text in reserved lower panel:

Pip wiggled his toes.  
Dot floated a leaf nearby.  
“I like this little bit,” said Pip.  
“Let’s play here.”
