# Character and style bible

## Pip
Small golden-brown bear cub, round ears, cream muzzle, dark brown eyes, small brown nose, short round limbs, three head-heights tall. Plain pale blue short-sleeved shirt, no shoes. Consistent rounded paws, no human fingers. Express worry through shoulders and eyebrows, then tentative enjoyment; never replace caution with fearless diving.

## Rue
Small ivory rabbit, dusty pink ear centers, dark brown eyes, pale sage pinafore. Same seated eye height as Pip; long ears make standing silhouette slightly taller. Patient smile, hands resting on her own lap or touching water; never pulls, pushes, lifts or holds Pip into water.

## Mama Bear
Adult warm brown bear, cream muzzle, plain muted terracotta cardigan, about twice Pip’s height. Sits within arm’s reach on the dry bank throughout; calm attentive presence, not the person choosing Pip’s pace.

## Style and continuity
Gentle translucent watercolor, warm paper texture, honey brown, pale blue, sage and soft terracotta. Soft morning light. One quiet, shallow clear pond edge with a gently sloped dry sandy bank; bottom visible immediately at the edge, no drop-off, waves or deep-water cues. Pip’s feet and bottom stay dry on the bank throughout; only paws touch water. Same seating arrangement: Pip nearest water, Rue beside him, Mama close behind. Clear focal gestures with uncluttered background. Coordinate paired artwork without claiming seamless continuous art.
