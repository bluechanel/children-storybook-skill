# Pip at the Water’s Edge

## 输入概况

小熊 Pip 怕水；朋友陪伴，从摸水面开始，最后自主选择在浅水边玩，仍允许害怕。

## 假设与状态

3 个主题跨页，6 个内页，加封面与封底；补充兔子朋友 Rue 和岸边照看的 Mama Bear。英文为 4 岁孩子朗读，温柔水彩。仅策划，所有图片未生成，项目不修改。

## Front cover

Pip at the Water’s Edge

## Page 1 — Just a Touch

Pip stayed on the dry bank. “The water makes me feel wobbly.” “We can sit here,” said his friend Rue.

## Page 2 — Just a Touch

Mama sat close by. Pip leaned toward the shallow edge. “Just one paw,” he said, and touched the cool water.

## Page 3 — A Little Ripple

A little ripple tickled Pip’s paw. He pulled it back. Rue waited beside him. “We can stop,” she said.

## Page 4 — A Little Ripple

Pip watched the water grow still. Then he dipped one paw again. “Look! I made a tiny circle.”

## Page 5 — My Kind of Splash

“I want to try a little splash,” said Pip. He stayed on the bank and patted the shallow water.

## Page 6 — My Kind of Splash

Pip splashed. Rue splashed beside him. “I still feel wobbly,” said Pip. “But I like playing right here.”
