# 逐页分镜

每跨页为相邻两张竖页；同一时空中展现连续动作，不是两张独立纸张。插画区 1024×900，下面保留单独排版的英文。人物脸和关键动作避开裁切边缘及内侧书沟。

## Spread 1: Just a Touch

情绪/主题：A safe place to begin。

### Left — Page 1

Pip stayed on the dry bank. “The water makes me feel wobbly.” “We can sit here,” said his friend Rue.

Action/composition: Pip sits on dry sandy bank, shoulders raised, watching clear shallow pond at right. Rue sits beside him without contact. Mama is close behind, within reach.

### Right — Page 2

Mama sat close by. Pip leaned toward the shallow edge. “Just one paw,” he said, and touched the cool water.

Action/composition: Same bank and arrangement. Pip independently leans slightly from a stable seat and touches water with one paw; feet and bottom remain dry. Rue waits; Mama watches calmly.

Continuity: Apply the fixed character bible; retain setting landmarks, clothing, scale and light across both pages.

Page-turn purpose: What will the first touch feel like?

## Spread 2: A Little Ripple

情绪/主题：Pause and choose。

### Left — Page 3

A little ripple tickled Pip’s paw. He pulled it back. Rue waited beside him. “We can stop,” she said.

Action/composition: Close view of Pip withdrawing a wet paw, a small ripple below. His face is unsure, not crying. Rue rests paws on her lap and gives space; Mama remains visibly close.

### Right — Page 4

Pip watched the water grow still. Then he dipped one paw again. “Look! I made a tiny circle.”

Action/composition: Same location. Pip reaches out again by choice, one paw making concentric ripples in transparent water. Gentle interest mixed with caution; Rue observes, no physical prompting.

Continuity: Apply the fixed character bible; retain setting landmarks, clothing, scale and light across both pages.

Page-turn purpose: What will Pip choose next?

## Spread 3: My Kind of Splash

情绪/主题：Play at his own pace。

### Left — Page 5

“I want to try a little splash,” said Pip. He stayed on the bank and patted the shallow water.

Action/composition: Pip remains firmly seated on dry bank and pats shallow water once with his paw, making a tiny splash. Rue smiles, Mama close behind.

### Right — Page 6

Pip splashed. Rue splashed beside him. “I still feel wobbly,” said Pip. “But I like playing right here.”

Action/composition: Pip and Rue seated side by side on dry bank, each patting the shallow water with one paw. Pip’s posture relaxes slightly yet retains tentative expression; Mama watches nearby.

Continuity: Apply the fixed character bible; retain setting landmarks, clothing, scale and light across both pages.

Page-turn purpose: Rest in a small success; feelings can remain.

## Covers

Back: A tiny ripple beside the dry sandy bank and two paw marks well away from the water, soft morning light, quiet simple vignette.
