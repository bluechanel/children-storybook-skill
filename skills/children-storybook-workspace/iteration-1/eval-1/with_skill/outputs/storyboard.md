# 逐页分镜

每跨页为相邻两张竖页；同一时空中展现连续动作，不是两张独立纸张。插画区 1024×900，下面保留单独排版的英文。人物脸和关键动作避开裁切边缘及内侧书沟。

## Spread 1: A Little Light

情绪/主题：Garden discovery。

### Left — Page 1

Milo found a tiny light under a leaf. “I am Glow,” it said. “I cannot find my home.”

Action/composition: Milo crouches on the left near a curled leaf; tiny Glow beneath it on the right. Close enough to see Glow’s lost expression; garden gate faintly behind Milo.

### Right — Page 2

“I will help,” said Milo. “What is your home like?” “Tall grass beside a little pond,” said Glow.

Action/composition: Continue same flower bed and dusk. Milo sits attentively, Glow hovers beside his face; distant grass shapes suggest the remembered home without depicting a thought bubble.

Continuity: Apply the fixed character bible; retain setting landmarks, clothing, scale and light across both pages.

Page-turn purpose: What does home look like?

## Spread 2: Looking Together

情绪/主题：A search, then a clue。

### Left — Page 3

They looked behind the flowers. No pond. They looked beside the carrots. No tall grass. Glow’s light grew small.

Action/composition: Milo and Glow search between flowers and carrots; frame both beds simply, no multiple copies of the characters. Glow hangs low, light modest, not extinguished.

### Right — Page 4

“We can rest,” said Milo. They sat beside a stone wall. Then Milo heard a soft sound. Plip. Plop.

Action/composition: Both rest at the stone wall. Milo tilts an ear toward the far side, Glow at his shoulder. Show one tiny ripple just glimpsed beyond stones as the water clue.

Continuity: Apply the fixed character bible; retain setting landmarks, clothing, scale and light across both pages.

Page-turn purpose: Will the sound lead them home?

## Spread 3: Over the Wall

情绪/主题：A shared way forward。

### Left — Page 5

“Water!” said Milo. He found a low gap in the wall. But the path beyond it was dark.

Action/composition: Milo discovers a low wall gap at right. Beyond lies a dim gentle path; Milo pauses with curiosity and uncertainty, not terror.

### Right — Page 6

“I can shine,” said Glow. Milo followed the little light. Together, they found the pond and its tall grass.

Action/composition: Glow flies slightly ahead, warm light falling onto the path. Milo follows through the gap; tall grass and pond appear at the destination. Same wall and lighting.

Continuity: Apply the fixed character bible; retain setting landmarks, clothing, scale and light across both pages.

Page-turn purpose: Who lives in the tall grass?

## Spread 4: Lights at Home

情绪/主题：Help comes home。

### Left — Page 7

Tiny lights blinked in the grass. “My family!” cried Glow. Milo smiled. Then he looked back at the dark path.

Action/composition: At the pond edge, Glow rejoins several family fireflies in tall grass. Milo stays on dry ground left, smiling while glancing at the returning path.

### Right — Page 8

“We will help you home,” said Glow. Little lights danced beside Milo, all the way to his garden gate.

Action/composition: Milo reaches the familiar garden gate, with Glow closest to him and family lights following. Warm farewell, matching gate from page 1; safe clear garden path.

Continuity: Apply the fixed character bible; retain setting landmarks, clothing, scale and light across both pages.

Page-turn purpose: A warm farewell; close the book.
