# Milo and the Little Light

## 输入概况

小兔 Milo 在花园遇到迷路的萤火虫，一起找家，朋友照亮回家的路。

## 假设与状态

4 个主题跨页，8 个内页，加封面与封底；角色名字补充为萤火虫 Glow。仅策划，全部图片缺失，未生成或接入。

## Front cover

Milo and the Little Light

## Page 1 — A Little Light

Milo found a tiny light under a leaf. “I am Glow,” it said. “I cannot find my home.”

## Page 2 — A Little Light

“I will help,” said Milo. “What is your home like?” “Tall grass beside a little pond,” said Glow.

## Page 3 — Looking Together

They looked behind the flowers. No pond. They looked beside the carrots. No tall grass. Glow’s light grew small.

## Page 4 — Looking Together

“We can rest,” said Milo. They sat beside a stone wall. Then Milo heard a soft sound. Plip. Plop.

## Page 5 — Over the Wall

“Water!” said Milo. He found a low gap in the wall. But the path beyond it was dark.

## Page 6 — Over the Wall

“I can shine,” said Glow. Milo followed the little light. Together, they found the pond and its tall grass.

## Page 7 — Lights at Home

Tiny lights blinked in the grass. “My family!” cried Glow. Milo smiled. Then he looked back at the dark path.

## Page 8 — Lights at Home

“We will help you home,” said Glow. Little lights danced beside Milo, all the way to his garden gate.
