# Character and style bible

## Milo
Small cream rabbit, round head, short plush body, long upright ears with dusty pink centers, dark brown eyes, tiny brown nose, fluffy round tail. Wears a plain moss-green vest, no shoes or other accessories. Three head-heights tall. Ear tips remain upright; mood changes through face and posture.

## Glow
Friendly tiny firefly with a dark brown oval head and thorax, honey-yellow glowing abdomen, two translucent oval wings, six delicate legs, two short antennae, two simple dark eyes. Body length equals Milo’s nose width; show closeups when needed without enlarging Glow relative to Milo in shared scenes. No clothing. Family has the same anatomy, subtler amber light; keep Glow identifiable nearest Milo.

## Style and continuity
Soft gouache/watercolor, rounded silhouettes, warm cream, moss green, muted lavender dusk, honey-yellow light. Friendly expressive faces; no ominous shadows. Garden flowers and carrots lead to a low grey stone wall with a rabbit-sized gap, then a pond fringed by tall grass. Dusk progresses slowly, same vest and character scale throughout. Pond discovery is beyond the wall, not suddenly inside the garden. Illustrations never contain type. Artwork is separate page art, coordinated across each spread, not a claimed seamless panorama.
