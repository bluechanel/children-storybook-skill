# Character settings

- **Milo:** small young cream-colored rabbit; two long upright ears with pale pink centers, round brown eyes, tiny cocoa nose, short round tail; wears the same plain moss-green neckerchief throughout. Gentle, observant, willing to ask for help. No clothing changes.
- **Little firefly:** tiny olive-brown oval body, two translucent wings, two short antennae, warm golden glowing abdomen; simple expressive dark eyes. Glow illuminates a small area, never a searchlight. Hovering body length about one tenth of Milo’s head height in shared shots. No clothes.
- **Family:** exactly three smaller background fireflies with the same anatomy and warm glow, appearing only on pages 6–7. The lead remains identifiable beside Milo.
- **Environment:** one connected garden path, tall wooden gate, pond and arching reed clump, rabbit home under a tree. Dusk gently deepens across the story.
- **Style:** soft watercolor, visible paper grain, rounded shapes, muted garden greens and lavender blues with warm yellow glows. Maintain anatomy, accessories, relative size and setting continuity across every image.
