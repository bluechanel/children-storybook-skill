# Page-by-page storyboard

Each theme occupies one open spread. Odd pages are left; even pages are right. Faces and essential actions stay away from the inner gutter.

## Page 1 · spread 1 · left · A Little Light

Wide view of a peaceful garden at dusk; Milo at left foreground bends toward a single glowing dot beneath a low leaf.

Text in reserved lower panel:

Milo hopped into the garden.  
A tiny light blinked under a leaf.

## Page 2 · spread 1 · right · A Little Light

Closer view of Milo sitting at the leaf; firefly hovering at his eye level; worried expression becoming curious.

Text in reserved lower panel:

“Hello,” said Milo. “Are you lost?”  
“Yes,” said the little firefly.  
“My home is by the pond.”

## Page 3 · spread 2 · left · Looking Together

Milo walking gently along the curved garden path, firefly hovering just ahead; flowers and a recognizable fork ahead.

Text in reserved lower panel:

“We can look together,” said Milo.  
They followed the garden path.  
Blink, blink went the little light.

## Page 4 · spread 2 · right · Looking Together

Both friends paused safely on path at fork, tall closed gate in distance left, subtle pond reflection beyond reeds right; no signage.

Text in reserved lower panel:

One path led to a tall gate.  
The other led to a soft splash.  
“Water!” said the firefly.

## Page 5 · spread 3 · left · A Familiar Place

Wide pond edge with dry safe path and several reed patches; both friends remain on land, searching.

Text in reserved lower panel:

They found the pond.  
But which reeds were home?  
The firefly looked all around.

## Page 6 · spread 3 · right · A Familiar Place

Three family fireflies blink above a distinctive arching reed clump; main firefly near Milo turns joyfully toward them.

Text in reserved lower panel:

“There!” said the firefly.  
Three little lights blinked in the reeds.  
“My family!”

## Page 7 · spread 4 · left · The Way Home

Family reunion above reeds at right, Milo on the same dry path at left looking toward garden path in deep blue dusk; peaceful, never threatening.

Text in reserved lower panel:

“Thank you, Milo!”  
The firefly flew to his family.  
Then Milo looked back.  
His path was dark.

## Page 8 · spread 4 · right · The Way Home

Firefly escorts Milo along path to warmly lit round rabbit home beneath garden tree; final pose at doorway, both smiling.

Text in reserved lower panel:

“I’ll light your way,” said his friend.  
Blink, blink, all the way to Milo’s door.  
“Good night, little light.”
