# Milo and the Little Light

English picture-book draft for ages 4–7. Planning only; all artwork is missing.

## Front cover

Milo and the Little Light

## Page 1 — A Little Light

Milo hopped into the garden.  
A tiny light blinked under a leaf.

## Page 2 — A Little Light

“Hello,” said Milo. “Are you lost?”  
“Yes,” said the little firefly.  
“My home is by the pond.”

## Page 3 — Looking Together

“We can look together,” said Milo.  
They followed the garden path.  
Blink, blink went the little light.

## Page 4 — Looking Together

One path led to a tall gate.  
The other led to a soft splash.  
“Water!” said the firefly.

## Page 5 — A Familiar Place

They found the pond.  
But which reeds were home?  
The firefly looked all around.

## Page 6 — A Familiar Place

“There!” said the firefly.  
Three little lights blinked in the reeds.  
“My family!”

## Page 7 — The Way Home

“Thank you, Milo!”  
The firefly flew to his family.  
Then Milo looked back.  
His path was dark.

## Page 8 — The Way Home

“I’ll light your way,” said his friend.  
Blink, blink, all the way to Milo’s door.  
“Good night, little light.”

## Back cover

A little light.  
A good friend.  
A way home.
