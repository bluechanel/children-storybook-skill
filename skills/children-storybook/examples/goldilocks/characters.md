# Characters and art direction

Shared art direction sits at the top; each `## ref-` section defines one reference sheet
that later pages reuse. `keywords` are how gen_art.py maps a prompt's reference lines
back to these files. See references/art-prompts.md.

size: 1536x1024
quality: high
style: warm hand-painted gouache and watercolour on textured cotton paper, rounded appealing silhouettes,
  expressive friendly faces, classic storybook illustration for ages 4-7, soft natural light, visible brush
  edges and paper grain.
framing: one coherent scene, the main subjects inside the central 75 percent of the frame with space above the heads
  and below the feet, because the left and right edges will be cropped. Faces away from the left and right
  edges. No collage, no split panels, no borders, no frames.
constraints: no text, letters, numbers, captions, signage, logos or watermarks; no extra characters; no extra limbs; no
  sharp teeth, no claws, no frightening shadows.
palette: moss and sage green, warm honey amber, cream paper, soft sky blue, butter yellow, rose pink, small
  strawberry-red accents.

## ref-goldilocks
keywords: Goldilocks
bible: Goldilocks, a human girl about six years old, golden-yellow springy curls gathered into two small bunches
  tied with one sky-blue ribbon, one loose curl over her forehead, round rosy cheeks, freckles across the
  nose, big hazel-brown eyes; soft butter-yellow dress with short puff sleeves, plain white pinafore apron,
  tiny strawberry-red bow at the collar, brown leather strap shoes.
sheet: A full-length character sheet of one girl on a plain warm cream paper background, shown standing and facing
  the viewer in a relaxed, friendly pose, hands holding a small bunch of wildflowers. Whole body visible from
  hair to shoes. She is Goldilocks, about six years old, with long GOLDEN-YELLOW hair, bright honey-gold, in
  soft springy curls gathered into two low bunches, each bunch tied with a SKY-BLUE ribbon, plus one loose
  golden curl over her forehead. Round rosy cheeks, freckles across the nose, big hazel-brown eyes, a small
  friendly smile. She wears a BUTTER-YELLOW dress with short puff sleeves and a plain WHITE pinafore apron
  over it, a tiny strawberry-red bow at the collar, and brown leather strap shoes with white socks. No
  cardigan, no jacket, no coat, no blue dress. No other figures, no props, no background scenery, no text.

## ref-bears
keywords: Papa Bear
bible: Papa Bear, the biggest and broadest, deep chestnut-brown fur, cream muzzle, chest and round belly, small
  calm dark eyes, thick brows, a dark moss-green waistcoat with two wooden buttons; Mama Bear, middle size and
  rounder, honey-cinnamon fur, cream muzzle and belly, a soft cream patch around one eye, warm dark eyes, a
  rose-pink apron with a small square pocket; Baby Bear, the smallest and roundest with a cub's big head and
  short legs, light golden-tan fur, cream muzzle and belly, a small tuft on the crown, very big round dark
  eyes, a sky-blue neckerchief tied at the side with one round wooden button. All three bears have friendly,
  gentle faces, never bared teeth and never a snarl.
sheet: A character sheet of three bears on a plain warm cream paper background, standing side by side and facing
  the viewer in friendly relaxed poses, shown from the biggest on the left to the smallest on the right so
  their very different sizes are obvious. Whole bodies visible from ears to feet. No other figures, no props,
  no background scenery, no text.
