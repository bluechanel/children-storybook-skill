## Brief

A familiar folktale retold for ages 4–7. Goldilocks wanders into the bears' cottage, tries
their porridge, chairs and beds, and is found asleep. This version resolves the fright with
an apology and a shared repair of the broken chair rather than a chase, so the child
protagonist acts and the ending is warm rather than punitive.

## Assumptions

- Audience: ages 4–7; read-aloud English.
- 11 thematic spreads / 22 interior pages, plus front and back covers.
- Art direction: warm hand-painted gouache and watercolour, rounded silhouettes, soft natural light.
- The page text below is the final delivered text, not a draft.

## Manuscript

**Cover** — Goldilocks and the Three Bears

### Spread 1 · A Door in the Wood

- left (page-01): One sunny morning, Goldilocks picked flowers at the edge of the wood. A blue butterfly danced past, and she ran after it, further and further from home.
- right (page-02): The butterfly flew away. Goldilocks looked up and saw a little cottage with a green door. She knocked. Nobody answered, so she pushed the door open.

### Spread 2 · Three Bowls of Porridge

- left (page-03): Inside, three bowls of warm porridge sat on the table. Goldilocks was very hungry. She tasted the biggest bowl. “Ouch! Too hot!”
- right (page-04): She tasted the middle bowl. “Too cold!” Then she tasted the smallest bowl. “Just right!” And she ate it all up, every single spoonful.

### Spread 3 · Three Chairs

- left (page-05): Next to the table stood three chairs. Goldilocks climbed onto the biggest chair, but it was far too tall. The middle chair was too wide and wobbly.
- right (page-06): The smallest chair was just right. She sat down and smiled — then crack! The little chair broke, and Goldilocks tumbled onto the floor.

### Spread 4 · Three Beds

- left (page-07): Upstairs she found three beds. The biggest bed was too hard. The middle bed was too soft. The smallest bed was just right, warm and cosy.
- right (page-08): Goldilocks pulled up the blanket, closed her eyes, and fell fast asleep. Soon the little bed was full of quiet, sleepy breathing.

### Spread 5 · The Bears Come Home

- left (page-09): The cottage belonged to three bears. They had gone for a walk to let their porridge cool. When they came home, they stood at the table and stared.
- right (page-10): “Someone has been eating my porridge!” said Papa Bear, in his big deep voice. “Someone has been eating my porridge too,” said Mama Bear.

### Spread 6 · Baby Bear's Chair

- left (page-11): Baby Bear looked at his little bowl. “Someone has eaten my porridge all up!” he said in his small voice. Then he saw the chairs.
- right (page-12): “Someone has been sitting in my chair,” said Papa Bear. “And someone has broken my chair!” said Baby Bear, and one big tear rolled down.

### Spread 7 · Up the Stairs

- left (page-13): The three bears climbed the stairs, soft and slow. Papa Bear looked at the biggest bed. “Someone has been sleeping in my bed!” he growled.
- right (page-14): Mama Bear looked at the middle bed. Baby Bear looked at his little bed — and there was Goldilocks, fast asleep under his blanket.

### Spread 8 · Don't Be Frightened

- left (page-15): Goldilocks woke up. She saw three bears looking at her, and she was very frightened. She sat up small and still, with her heart going thump, thump, thump.
- right (page-16): “Don't be frightened, little one,” said Mama Bear softly. “We will not hurt you.” Papa Bear nodded. Baby Bear peeped out from behind his mother.

### Spread 9 · I Am Sorry

- left (page-17): Goldilocks took a deep breath. “I am sorry,” she said. “I ate your porridge and I broke your little chair. I should have knocked first.”
- right (page-18): “I liked that chair,” said Baby Bear sadly. “Then let's mend it together,” said Goldilocks. “I can hold the pieces while you tap the nails.”

### Spread 10 · Mending Together

- left (page-19): Papa Bear brought wood and a pot of glue. Goldilocks held the legs, Baby Bear tapped, and Mama Bear tied the seat with string. Tap, tap, click!
- right (page-20): The little chair stood up again, strong as new. Mama Bear cooked more porridge, and everyone sat down to share it — three bears and one new friend.

### Spread 11 · Home Again

- left (page-21): When the sun began to set, Goldilocks walked home through the wood. “Next time I will knock and wait,” she said. The three bears waved from the door.
- right (page-22): Mama was waiting with a warm hug. “Did you have an adventure?” she asked. “Yes,” said Goldilocks. “And I made three new friends.”
