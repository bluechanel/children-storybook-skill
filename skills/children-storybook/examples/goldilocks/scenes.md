# Scenes

One `## <name>` section per generated asset, in generation order: cover, back, then
the interior pages. `refs` lists the reference sheets in the order the model receives
them. See references/art-prompts.md.

## cover

scene: Front cover illustration. Goldilocks stands in a sunlit woodland clearing in front of a small honey-stone
  cottage with a green plank door, holding a bunch of wildflowers and looking up at the three friendly bears
  who stand together in the doorway. Warm morning light, a generous empty band of soft sky and leaves across
  the top of the frame.
